怎么做四步

公开数据

冲突、灾害、断网、新闻、港口和口岸、贸易、中国融资项目、经济指标，按时自动更新。

种子文档

按国家整理成文档，写清楚涉及哪些真实的人和机构，每条注明来源。你也可以上传自己的资料。

群体推演

推演引擎建知识图谱，生成大量智能体，在模拟社交平台上互相影响，一轮一轮演化。

推演报告

输出报告和全过程数据（PDF、Word、Markdown、CSV），看清各方会怎么反应、风险在哪里。

适合什么样的决策

海外投资与项目落地

在某国建电站、修铁路、开矿、设厂之前，看当地政府、社区、媒体、竞争对手可能的反应。

例：在巴基斯坦扩建电站，当地舆论和地方政府会怎么看？
进入新市场

新产品、新渠道进入一个国家，消费者、经销商、监管部门的接受度。

例：电动车进入中亚市场，哪类人群会先接受？
政策与监管变化

关税、制裁、出口管制、本地化要求出台后，对项目和贸易的连锁影响。

例：某国提高矿产出口税，承包商和下游买家会怎么调整？
供应链与物流中断

港口拥堵、口岸关闭、航线改道、自然灾害时，各方的应对和替代路线。

例：某口岸关闭两周，班列和货主会转向哪里？
舆情与危机公关

事故、劳资纠纷、环保争议发生后，不同回应方式会引发什么样的舆论。

例：项目工地出事故，先道歉还是先说明？
谈判与合作

合作方、东道国政府、金融机构在不同条件下的立场和底线。

例：提出延期还款，贷款方和当地政府各自的态度？
研究与政策评估

智库、高校、政府部门评估一项倡议或政策在多国的反响。

例：一项绿色融资新规在东南亚各国的反应差异。
技术与研发方向

新技术、新标准推广时，产业链各方的接受度和阻力（偏方向性判断）。

例：数据中心落地某国，电力部门和社区的顾虑。

我们不做什么

推演是模拟，不是预言。它展示的是“可能的走向”和“各方可能的反应”，不保证结果，也不是投资、法律或安全建议。种子数据来自公开来源，以原始来源为准；重要决策请结合实地调研和专业意见。

How it worksfour steps

Public data

Conflict, hazards, outages, news, ports and crossings, trade, Chinese-financed projects, economic indicators — updated automatically.

Seed documents

Organised by country, naming the real people and organisations involved, every fact sourced. You can add your own files.

Swarm simulation

The engine builds a knowledge graph and many agents that influence each other on simulated social platforms, round by round.

Report

A report plus the full process data (PDF, Word, Markdown, CSV): how each side may react and where the risks are.

Decisions it helps with

Overseas investment and projects

Before building a plant, railway, mine or factory: how government, communities, media and competitors may respond.

Entering a new market

How consumers, distributors and regulators may receive a new product or channel.

Policy and regulatory change

Knock-on effects of tariffs, sanctions, export controls or localisation rules on projects and trade.

Supply chain and logistics disruption

Port congestion, border closures, rerouting or disasters: how actors respond and which routes they switch to.

Reputation and crisis response

After an accident, labour dispute or environmental controversy: how different responses may play out.

Negotiation and partnership

Positions and red lines of partners, host governments and lenders under different terms.

Research and policy evaluation

Think tanks, universities and agencies testing how an initiative may be received across countries.

Technology direction

Acceptance and resistance across the value chain when new technology or standards roll out (directional).

What we don't do

A simulation is not a prophecy. It shows possible paths and how actors may react; it does not guarantee outcomes and is not investment, legal or security advice. Seed data comes from public sources, which prevail. For important decisions, combine it with field work and professional advice.
