## 中国方面的出资方和企业（AidData 项目标题原文统计，2000–2021 年承诺）

- 中国进出口银行（China Eximbank）：在泰国出现在 6 个项目中，涉及金额约 4.66 亿美元。例：2013 年 China Eximbank provides loan worth THB 11.6 billion for Chao Phraya Estate Landmark Waterfront Construction Pr；2010 年 China Eximbank provides $17 million loan for Phase 2 of Thai-Chinese Rayong Industrial Zone Construction Proje；2006 年 China Eximbank provides RMB 50 million loan for Phase 1 of Thai-Chinese Rayong Industrial Zone Construction Pr
- 国家开发银行（China Development Bank）：在泰国出现在 2 个项目中，涉及金额约 1.68 亿美元。例：2020 年 China Development Bank contributes to $250 million syndicated loan for Extension of Petroleum Pipeline System ；2012 年 CDB provides $24 million loan for Bangkok Nong Khaem Municipal Solid Waste Incineration Power Plant
- 中国工商银行（ICBC）：在泰国出现在 2 个项目中，涉及金额约 1.21 亿美元。例：2018 年 ICBC provides $97.5 million loan for 2,500MW Gulf Sriracha Power Plant Project；2012 年 ICBC contributes THB 379,250,000 to syndicated loan for Korat 3, Korat 4 and Korat 7 Solar Power Plant Constru
- Chinese Ministry of Education：在泰国出现在 1 个项目中，涉及金额约 383 万美元。例：2000 年 Chinese Ministry of Education provides THB 60 million grant for Chinese language and cultural center at Mae Fa
- 中国政府：在泰国出现在 9 个项目中，涉及金额约 159 万美元。例：2010 年 China commits 6.7 million RMB for the Traditional Medical Center Construction Project；2009 年 China donates 4 million Baht to fire victims in Thailand；2017 年 Chinese Government provides disaster relief materials worth THB 1 million to Thailand following Jan 2017 flood
- 中国驻当地使领馆：在泰国出现在 3 个项目中，涉及金额约 3 万美元。例：2016 年 Embassy of China in Thailand grants 150 scholarships totaling 750,000 THB for students in Young Ambassadors of；2020 年 The Chinese Embassy in Thailand donates masks to Thai government institutions and hospitals；2021 年 Chinese Embassy in Thailand donates two nucleic acid testing vehicles to Thai Ministry of Health in July 2021
- 汉办 / 孔子学院总部（Hanban）：在泰国出现在 13 个项目中。例：2015 年 Hanban provides grant for the establishment of Maritime Silk Road Confucius Institute at Dhurakij Pundit Unive；2015 年 Hanban provides grant for the establishment of Confucius Institute at Assumption University；2009 年 Hanban provides grant for the establishment of Confucius Institute at Burapha University
- Overseas Chinese Affairs Office：在泰国出现在 2 个项目中。例：2009 年 Overseas Chinese Affairs Office sends traditional Chinese physicians to Thailand in March 2009；2012 年 Overseas Chinese Affairs Office sends traditional Chinese physicians to Thailand in March 2012
- Consulate General of China in Chiang Mai：在泰国出现在 2 个项目中。例：2020 年 Consulate General of China in Chiang Mai donates masks to Thai Senate for epidemic prevention in Thailand in 2；2020 年 Consulate General of China in Chiang Mai donates funds and medical masks to Chiang Mai province for wildfires
