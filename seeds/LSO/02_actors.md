- 中国进出口银行（China Eximbank）：在莱索托出现在 1 个项目中，涉及金额约 1.15 亿美元。例：2018 年 China Eximbank provides RMB 700 million government concessional loan for Ha Mpiti to Sehlaba-Thebe Road Projec
- 中国援外医疗队：在莱索托出现在 3 个项目中，涉及金额约 9 万美元。例：2018 年 Chinese 13th medical team donates medical supplies worth 450,000 M to Motebang Hospital in Lesotho (Linked to ；2017 年 Chinese 12th medical team donates medical equipment worth 370,000 LSL to Lesotho (Linked to #55839)；2016 年 Chinese 11th Medical Team donates medicine and equipment worth 300,000 LSL to Lesotho (linked to #55842)
- 中国驻当地使领馆：在莱索托出现在 5 个项目中，涉及金额约 3 万美元。例：2018 年 Chinese Embassy in Lesotho awards M198,000 to 6 Basotho entrepreneurs；2017 年 Chinese Embassy donates medical equipment worth 100,000 M to the Motebang Hospital in Lesotho；2018 年 Chinese Embassy in Lesotho awards up to M53,500 to 35 Basotho students for story writing competition
